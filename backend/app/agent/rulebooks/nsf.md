# NSF rulebook

The rules the agent applies to a National Science Foundation (NSF) grant, beyond the checks code runs. The agent gets this whole file on every run. When an answer or a flag rests on a rule here, the answer names the rule's ID.

Each rule below was checked against its source on the date it shows. A rule goes in only after that check. Changing a rule is a change to this file.

**Sources and editions.**

- 2 CFR 200 (Title 2 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Part 200), the federal Uniform Guidance, as published on the Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR), current as of November 6, 2026.
- NSF's Grant General Conditions (GC-1), the edition dated July 31, 2026. GC-1 numbers its parts "Term and Condition No.". An award notice states which conditions govern it and their date.
- NSF's Proposal and Award Policies and Procedures Guide (PAPPG), NSF 24-1, with Supplement 1 (NSF 26-200, for awards made on or after December 8, 2025) and Supplement 2 (NSF 26-202, for awards made on or after January 22, 2026, on public access and data sharing).

Where a rule says "the university", it means the recipient of the award.

**How a rule's kind decides the answer.** Each rule sits under one of three kinds, and each rule repeats its kind on a `- Kind:` line. A not-allowed rule gives contradicts when the documents show that kind of cost, unless they show the exception the rule states. An allowed-under-a-condition rule gives supports when a document shows the condition is met, and contradicts when no document does. A judgment rule gives a flag, not contradicts, unless the rule itself names a comparison that gives contradicts.

Parts of 200.431, 200.435, 200.446, 200.449, 200.450, 200.464, 200.465 and 200.470 are unread too. Every section from 200.420 to 200.476 was searched for "unallowable" and "not allowable", and each hit was read.
- NSF PAPPG 24-1: every chapter except Chapter X and Chapter II.D.2.f(v) and II.D.2.f(xiii) is unread, including Chapter VI.D.3.c on further extensions. Of Supplement 1, only its list of changes and items 4 and 13 were read. Supplement 2 was read in full.
- NSF GC-1 beyond Term and Condition Nos. 1 to 12.
- NSF's Prior Approval Matrix, and the Research Terms and Conditions that govern awards made before October 1, 2024.

A cost that no rule here names isn't thereby allowed. The agent still answers whether it is necessary, reasonable and allocable to the award.

## Kinds of cost allowed only under a condition

### NSF-AIRFARE-CLASS

Airfare above the basic least expensive unrestricted class is not allowed. A higher class is allowed only when the cheaper one would need circuitous routing, travel at unreasonable hours, excessively long travel, extra costs that offset the savings, or would not meet the traveler's medical needs, and the university justifies and documents that reason for the trip.

- Kind: allowed under a condition
- Source: [2 CFR 200.475(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.475#p-200.475(e))
- Source: [NSF GC-1 (July 31, 2026), Term and Condition No. 10.a.2](https://nsf-gov-resources.nsf.gov/files/gc1_7_31_2026.pdf)
- Quote: "the difference between economy airfare and a higher-class airfare is unallowable" (GC-1 No. 10.a.2)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-US-FLAG-CARRIER

Air travel to, from, between or within countries other than the U.S. paid with NSF funds must be on a U.S.-flag air carrier, or a code-share with one, when one is available, and the ticket must show the U.S. carrier's code and flight number. A foreign carrier is allowed only under GC-1's listed exceptions: an Open Skies agreement (such as the one with the European Union), involuntary rerouting by the U.S. carrier, the listed time and connection limits (for example, a U.S. carrier would add 24 hours or more to a trip to or from the U.S.), and short trips of three hours or less that a U.S. carrier would at least double; lower cost or convenience is not an exception.

- Kind: allowed under a condition
- Source: [NSF GC-1 (July 31, 2026), Term and Condition No. 10.c and 10.d](https://nsf-gov-resources.nsf.gov/files/gc1_7_31_2026.pdf)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-MEALS-NOT-TRAVELING

Travel, meal and hotel costs of university employees who are not on travel status are not allowed. The policy guide also says no funds may be requested for meals or coffee breaks at internal meetings of the university or its parts, such as labs, departments and centers; that is proposal guidance and doesn't on its own make such a meal contradicts, unless the award notice restates it as a condition of the award, which makes it one of the grant's terms.

- Kind: allowed under a condition
- Source: [NSF PAPPG 24-1, Chapter X.C.3](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-10-allowability-of-costs)
- Source: [NSF PAPPG 24-1, Chapter II.D.2.f(xiii)(a) and (b)](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-2-proposal-preparation)
- Quote: "Travel, meal, and hotel expenses of recipient employees who are not on travel status are unallowable." (PAPPG X.C.3)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-PUBLICATION-FEES

Page charges, article processing charges and open access fees are allowed only when the publication reports work the federal award supported and the journal charges them on every item it publishes, federally funded or not. Publication costs not incurred during the period of performance may be charged during closeout, to the final budget period.

- Kind: allowed under a condition
- Source: [2 CFR 200.461(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.461#p-200.461(b))
- Source: [NSF PAPPG 24-1, Chapter X.A.2.c, Post-End Date Costs](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-10-allowability-of-costs)
- Source: [NSF PAPPG 24-1, Supplement 2 (NSF 26-202), item 1, revising Chapter XI.D.2.c](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/document/pappg24-1-supplement-2)
- Quote: "Special fees for the purpose of making published versions of record (VORs) publicly accessible are allowable costs but such payments are not required for deposits of AAMs in PAR." (PAPPG 24-1 Supplement 2)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-POST-END-COMMITMENTS

NSF funds may not be spent after the award's end date, except to pay valid commitments made on or before the end date, such as a contractor's or subrecipient's services during the award period that were billed after it ended. Equipment or materials and supplies ordered after the end date generally may not be charged; the agent cites this rule when it disputes the date check with proof that the order or commitment was made by the end date.

- Kind: allowed under a condition
- Source: [NSF PAPPG 24-1, Chapter X.A.2.c, Post-End Date Costs](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-10-allowability-of-costs)
- Quote: "NSF funds may not be expended subsequent to the end date of the award, except to liquidate valid commitments that were made on or before the end date" (PAPPG X.A.2.c)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-CONSULTANTS

Consultant and professional service costs are allowed only for people who are not officers or employees of the university, when the cost is reasonable for the service and not contingent on recovering costs from NSF. The university must be able to justify that the rate of pay is reasonable.

- Kind: allowed under a condition
- Source: [NSF GC-1 (July 31, 2026), Term and Condition No. 7](https://nsf-gov-resources.nsf.gov/files/gc1_7_31_2026.pdf)
- Source: [2 CFR 200.459(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.459#p-200.459(a))
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-ADMIN-SALARIES

Administrative and clerical staff salaries are normally indirect costs. They may be charged directly only when all three hold: the services are integral to the award, the people can be specifically identified with the award, and the costs are not also recovered as indirect costs.

- Kind: allowed under a condition
- Source: [2 CFR 200.413(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.413#p-200.413(c))
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-VISAS

Short-term visas are generally allowed and may be charged directly when they are critical and necessary for the project, allowable, consistent with the university's written policy, and meet the definition of a direct cost. Longer-term immigration visas are not covered by this allowance.

- Kind: allowed under a condition
- Source: [2 CFR 200.463(d)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.463#p-200.463(d))
- Quote: "Short-term visas (as opposed to longer-term immigration visas) are generally an allowable cost" (2 CFR 200.463(d))
- Checked: 2026-11-10

## Kinds of cost that are not allowed

Some of these have a narrow exception, written into the rule. Where the exception needs something "included in the award", the award notice or its approved budget has to show it.

### NSF-NEVER-ALCOHOL

Alcoholic beverages are not allowed.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.423](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.423)
- Source: [NSF PAPPG 24-1, Chapter II.D.2.f(xiii)(c)](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-2-proposal-preparation)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-PROMOTIONAL

Promotional items and memorabilia are not allowed, and neither is advertising or public relations meant only to promote the university. Gifts, t-shirts and memorabilia for participants are a separate case: they need NSF's prior written approval as participant support.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.421(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.421#p-200.421(e))
- Source: [NSF GC-1 (July 31, 2026), Term and Condition No. 3.a](https://nsf-gov-resources.nsf.gov/files/gc1_7_31_2026.pdf)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-ENTERTAINMENT

Entertainment, amusement, diversion and social activities, and costs tied to them such as tickets, meals, lodging, transportation and gifts, are not allowed. The only exception is a cost with a specific and direct programmatic purpose that is included in the award.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.438(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.438#p-200.438(a))
- Source: [NSF PAPPG 24-1, Chapter II.D.2.f(xiii)(a)](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-2-proposal-preparation)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-DONATIONS

Contributions and donations from the university to other entities, in cash, property or services, are not allowed.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.434(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.434#p-200.434(a))
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-FINES

Fines, penalties, damages and settlements from the university's violation, alleged violation or failure to comply with laws and regulations are not allowed. The exceptions are costs that come from complying with specific provisions of the award, or that have NSF's prior written approval.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.441](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.441)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-FUNDRAISING

Organized fundraising, such as financial campaigns, endowment drives and soliciting gifts, is not allowed, and neither is investment counsel to increase investment income. Fundraising to meet the program's objectives is allowed only with NSF's prior written approval.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.442(a) and (b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.442)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-LOBBYING

Lobbying is not allowed: any attempt to influence elections or legislation, any attempt to improperly influence a federal award decision, and membership in organizations whose main purpose is lobbying. Technical and factual presentations on the award's topic made at a documented request of a legislature are one of the listed exceptions, but their travel, lodging and meals stay unallowable unless they are for testimony at a regularly scheduled Congressional hearing on the written request of the committee's Chairman or Ranking Minority Member.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.450(b), (c)(1) and (c)(2)(i)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.450)
- Source: [2 CFR 200.454(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.454#p-200.454(e))
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-PERSONAL-USE

Goods or services for the personal use of the university's employees are not allowed, whether or not they are reported as taxable income. Housing and personal living expenses for employees are allowed only as direct costs approved in advance by NSF.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.445](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.445)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-INTEREST

Interest on borrowed money, on temporary use of endowment funds, or on the university's own funds is not allowed. The exception is financing costs to buy, build or replace capital assets, under the conditions in 2 CFR 200.449.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.449(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.449#p-200.449(a))
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-SOCIAL-CLUBS

Membership in a country club or a social or dining club is not allowed. Membership in business, technical, professional, civic or community organizations is allowed.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.454(a), (c) and (d)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.454)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-ALUMNI

Costs of alumni activities, or in support of them, are not allowed.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.424](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.424)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-STUDENT-ACTIVITIES

Intramural activities, student publications, student clubs and other student activities are not allowed unless the award expressly authorizes them.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.469](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.469)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-COMMENCEMENT

Commencement and convocation costs are not allowed.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.429](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.429)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-COVERED-TELECOM

Award funds may not buy, or renew a contract for, covered telecommunications or video surveillance equipment or services: telecommunications equipment made by Huawei or ZTE, certain video surveillance and telecommunications equipment made by Hytera, Hikvision or Dahua, and services that use them, including their subsidiaries and affiliates. This also covers any system that uses such equipment as a substantial or essential component.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.216](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.216)
- Source: [2 CFR 200.471](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.471)
- Source: [NSF PAPPG 24-1, Chapter X.F](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-10-allowability-of-costs)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-COVERED-DRONES

On or after December 22, 2025, NSF funds may not buy drones (unmanned aircraft systems) made or assembled by a covered foreign entity, or pay for operating one. Covered sources include entities on the Consolidated Screening List and entities from, or controlled by, the People's Republic of China, as the Secretary of Homeland Security determines.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [NSF PAPPG 24-1, Supplement 1 (NSF 26-200), item 4, adding Chapter II.D.2.f(xiii)(f)](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/document/pappg24-1-supplement-1)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-EXEMPT-TAXES

Taxes the university could have been exempted from are not allowed: an exemption available to it directly, or one based on the federal government's exemption when the federal agency makes the needed exemption certificates available. Federal income taxes and special assessments on land for capital improvements are not allowed either.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.470(b)(1)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.470#p-200.470(b)(1))
- Quote: "Taxes for which exemptions are available to the recipient or subrecipient directly or which are available to the recipient or subrecipient based on an exemption afforded the Federal Government and, in the latter case, when the Federal agency makes available the necessary exemption certificates" (2 CFR 200.470(b)(1)(i))
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-AFFILIATED-RENTAL

Renting property owned by people or entities affiliated with the university, such as for a home office, is not allowed.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.465(f)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.465#p-200.465(f))
- Source: [NSF PAPPG 24-1, Chapter II.D.2.f(xiii)(d)](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-2-proposal-preparation)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-NEVER-OTHER-AWARD-LOSSES

Costs over income on any other award are not allowed, and neither are costs moved from another award because they exceeded that award's funding. A cost transfer whose only reason is that the other award ran out of money falls under this rule.

- Kind: not allowed
- Source: [2 CFR 200.451](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.451)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

## Judgment rules the agent flags or compares

### NSF-END-DATE-PURCHASES

The university typically should not buy equipment or computing devices, or restock materials and supplies, to use up remaining funds when little or no time is left before the end date to use them in the project. NSF names no number of days and doesn't call this unallowable, so the agent flags such a charge rather than answering contradicts.

- Kind: judgment
- Source: [NSF PAPPG 24-1, Chapter X.A.2.c, Post-End Date Costs](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-10-allowability-of-costs)
- Quote: "the recipient typically should not purchase items of equipment, computing devices, or restocking of materials and supplies" (PAPPG X.A.2.c)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-PARTICIPANT-NOT-STAFF

Participant support costs are for participants, such as trainees, students or conference attendees, not for people who develop or deliver the award's activities: consultants, project personnel and the university's staff are not participants. Speakers and trainers generally aren't participants either, unless their main purpose at the event is to learn as a participant. The agent compares who was paid with the people named on the grant: a match is contradicts, citing both documents, and someone not named on the grant is supports.

- Kind: judgment
- Source: [2 CFR 200.1, definitions of Participant and Participant support costs](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.1)
- Source: [NSF PAPPG 24-1, Chapter II.D.2.f(v)](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-2-proposal-preparation)
- Quote: "Individuals committing effort to the development or delivery of program activities under a Federal award (such as consultants, project personnel, or staff members of a recipient or subrecipient) are not participants." (2 CFR 200.1)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-LATE-EXTENSION

The university may extend the end date once, by up to 12 months, on its own, but it must notify NSF through Research.gov, with its reasons and the new period, at least ten calendar days before the end date. Extensions beyond that authority follow the procedures in PAPPG Chapter VI.D.3.c (GC-1 No. 6.c), which this rulebook hasn't read, so a notice filed later than ten days, or a further extension, is flagged rather than judged. The one-time extension may not be used merely to spend the remaining balance, and an award with a zero balance can't be extended; the agent flags a notice that shows any of these.

- Kind: judgment
- Source: [NSF GC-1 (July 31, 2026), Term and Condition No. 6.a to 6.c and footnote 4](https://nsf-gov-resources.nsf.gov/files/gc1_7_31_2026.pdf)
- Source: [2 CFR 200.308(g)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.308#p-200.308(g)(2))
- Quote: "this one-time extension may not be exercised merely for the purpose of using any unliquidated balances" (GC-1 No. 6.a)
- Quote: "The recipient is not authorized to extend an award that contains a zero balance." (GC-1 No. 6, footnote 4)
- Checked: 2026-11-10

### NSF-FRINGE-ON-STIPENDS

Fringe benefits follow the base that the university's own negotiated rate agreement names, so a fringe charge on a stipend is questionable when a rate term on the grant states the base as salaries and wages. This comes from one audit finding, not a written rule, and the university partly disputes it, so the agent flags the charge and never answers contradicts; the rule applies only when a rate term on the grant states its base.

- Kind: judgment
- Source: [NSF Office of Inspector General, Performance Audit of Incurred Costs, Cornell University, OIG 26-01-001 (March 9, 2026), Unallowable Fringe Benefits and footnote 18](https://www.oversight.gov/sites/default/files/documents/reports/2026-03/26-01-001%20Performance%20Audit%20of%20Incurred%20Costs-%20Cornell%20University-Final_0.pdf)
- Source: [2 CFR 200.431(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/section-200.431#p-200.431(a))
- Quote: "Cornell's Negotiated Indirect Cost Rate Agreement (NICRA), dated June 30, 2022, Section I: Fringe Benefit Rates, states that Cornell's fringe benefit rate base consists of salaries and wages." (OIG 26-01-001, footnote 18)
- Checked: 2026-11-10
