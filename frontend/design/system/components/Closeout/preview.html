Berkeley’s closeout steps

From Contracts and Grants Accounting (CGA), for an end date of Jan 31, 2027
  1. Apr 12, 2026Today
  2. Nov 2, 2026Notice of ExpirationCGA sends it 90, 60 and 30 days before the end date
  3. Jan 31, 2027End dateNo new spending. Adjustments until the books close
  4. Your deadlineMar 31, 2027Award Closeout Certification dueYou file it in BFS (Berkeley Financial System) with the ledger report
  5. May 1, 2027Final financial reportDay 90. CGA enters the final amount in the Award Cash Management Service (ACM$) for NSF
  6. May 31, 2027Day 120Anything missed needs a Closeout Exception Request
