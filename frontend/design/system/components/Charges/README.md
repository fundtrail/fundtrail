# Charges

Every charge on the grant, not just the problem list. It shares the grant page's frame: the grant band with its readiness, then the list on the left and the selected charge on the right.

**The list.** Bucket tabs with counts (All, Possible conflict, Needs a human, Missing evidence, Complete), a search over description, vendor, ledger line and amount, and the three budget lines above the split, which double as a filter (budget, charged and what's left on each). Newest first; Date and Amount sort from their headers. Problem rows keep their bucket badge and say why in one line; Evidence complete is a quiet label with the vendor and budget line, so color stays where something needs the accountant. The list scrolls inside its panel with its header pinned.

**The charge.** The same evidence trail as the problem list, in the same order and style. Link names and quotes open the viewer at their passage (the six story charges; the 45 generated charges have no original in the demo). Then, following PR 6 (not merged yet): **Evidence needed**, the charge's checklist (`FTChecklist`; the demo's default for every account is "A bill that adds up to the charge", and L-0981 adds the program officer's approval, added by code), with **Bills covering this charge** right under it: each file that pays for or approves the charge, with how much of it this charge uses ("$2,180.00 of $2,180.00"). A filled bill item stays one short line, since the bills list names the file and the amount. Then the charge's **thread** (`FTThread`): code's reasons, the agent's comments and the accountant's decisions, with a box to ask the agent about this charge (scripted answers in the demo; each charge keeps its thread while you move between rows). Then the history, folded. Answering the agent and entering a split stay on the problem list; here those buttons say so.

**Data.** Built by `tools/charges/gen.py` from the report data (`report-data.json`): all 51 charges, totaling $96,412.18, with each budget line's total checked against the grant's. The six charges the demo tells a story about carry the Demo's end state on Apr 12, 2026.

**Keyboard.** Rows are focusable; up and down arrows, Home and End move the selection; Enter selects. Left and right arrows move between the bucket tabs.

**Hash params, for screenshots:** `#f=human` (a bucket), `#line=ms|odc|psc`, `#sel=L-0347`, `#q=pipette`, `#sort=amount`, `#t=dark`.

**The header shows both sides of the budget line** (PR 6): the ledger's own account and the agent's budget line. In the demo ledger they always agree; when they disagree, the header says so and the charge waits for you. Photos say "box checked by a second look", not an approximate area.

**Layout (the standard, option C).** Below the grant header, the charge list and the selected charge fill the rest of the screen and scroll separately; the page itself doesn't scroll. The charge's details scroll in the top of the right panel, and its review thread is docked at the bottom: newest entry last, scrolled to it, with the ask box always on screen. The dock takes 42% of the panel, or 67% with Show more. On short screens (a panel under 640px tall) it starts at about a third, never less than the newest entry plus the ask box, so the details keep more room. Picking another row slides the new details in quietly (none under reduced motion) and the dock switches to that charge's thread. Below 1024px wide the page falls back to normal scrolling. The layout script is built into this card by `tools/inline_layout.py` in the design system's tooling, and `tools/charges/gen.py` runs it on every rebuild.
