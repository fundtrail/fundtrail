How to read this report

  • Checks are run by code on the records: dates against the period, each charge's evidence checklist and whether its bills add up, the approver's role and channel, and budget room.
  • Answers to questions code can't settle are labeled with who wrote them, Agent or Accountant, with their reasoning. The accountant has the last word; their decisions show on the charge they changed, and conversations with the agent stay out.
  • Quotes are checked against their file: the exact line, the passage on its PDF page, or on photos a box checked by a second look. A fact that couldn't be confirmed says so.
  • Pages 2 and 3 list every charge. Pages 4 to 6 show the checks and evidence charge by charge. Page 7 is the sign-off, and pages 8 and 9 list every source file.
