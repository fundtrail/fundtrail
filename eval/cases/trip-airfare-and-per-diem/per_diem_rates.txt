UNIVERSITY OF EXAMPLE
Department of Earth Sciences
Field Research Per Diem

Effective date: July 1, 2025

Travel to an approved field site is reimbursed at a flat per diem in place
of itemized lodging and meal receipts. The per diem covers lodging, meals
and incidental expenses at the field site.

Field site per diem: $100.00 per day

The per diem is paid for each day of the trip, including the days of
departure and return. Airfare, rental vehicles and registration fees are
reimbursed separately against receipts.

Approved field sites for this rate include Example Creek Field Station.

Questions: Earth Sciences business office, travel@university.example
