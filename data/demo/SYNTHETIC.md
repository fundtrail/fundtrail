# Synthetic data notice

Every file in this folder was written for the FundTrail hackathon demo. No document here was issued by any agency or institution, and no person, award number, or email address is real. Award numbers use the `SYN-` prefix and email addresses use the reserved `.example` domain.

The rule documents that quote NSF Grant General Conditions (GC-1) or the NSF Prior Approval Matrix are excerpts of public federal text, cited to their source.
